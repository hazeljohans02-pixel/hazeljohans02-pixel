Frequently Asked

Common Questions

Orders & Delivery

How long does delivery take?

Most appliances are delivered within 5–7 business days of your order being confirmed. Larger items like refrigerators may take up to 9 days in select cities.

Can I track my order?

Yes — use the order tracker above with your order ID and the phone number or email you used at checkout.

Do you deliver to my city?

We currently deliver and install in 50+ cities. Enter your pincode at checkout to confirm availability for your area.

Installation

Is installation really free?

Yes. Every appliance we sell includes free installation by a certified Voltrix technician — no hidden charges, no upsells.

How do I schedule installation?

Once your order is delivered, you'll get an SMS and email link to pick a convenient slot, usually within 48 hours of delivery.

What if I need to reschedule?

You can reschedule up to 2 hours before your appointment using the link sent in your confirmation message.

Warranty & Repairs

What does the 2-year warranty cover?

Full parts and labor coverage for manufacturing defects. Wear-and-tear items like filters and belts are excluded — see your product's warranty card for details.

How do I file a warranty claim?

Raise a request from your account dashboard, or call support with your order ID and a description of the issue.

Do you offer extended warranty?

Yes — extended plans of up to 5 years are available at checkout for most major appliances.

Returns & Refunds

What is your return window?

We offer a 7-day replacement window from the date of delivery for manufacturing defects or damage in transit.

How long do refunds take?

Once a return is approved, refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method.

Can I exchange for a different model?

Yes, subject to the price difference, as long as it's within the 7-day return window and the original unit is unused.
